OUR STORY

Adventure Was Never Just A Business.

Bhrighu Adventure began in Manali in 2019 with a simple idea — to share the mountains through experiences built from real time spent outdoors.

The journey grew from trekking, climbing and mountaineering into a wider range of Himalayan adventures — from rock faces and high-altitude trails to snow, remote valleys and mountain roads.

Manali remains our base and the Himalayas remain at the centre of everything we do.

HOW WE THINK

Our Mountain Philosophy.

01

Experience First

An adventure should be remembered for more than reaching a destination. The journey, landscape and time spent in the mountains matter just as much.

02

Respect The Mountains

Weather, terrain and mountain conditions are always part of the decision-making process. Plans may change when the mountains demand it.

03

Keep It Personal

Different people come to the mountains with different goals. We believe the experience should reflect the people, conditions and journey involved.

04

Keep Exploring

The Himalayas are much bigger than one trail or one summit. Every valley, season and route offers something new to discover.

FOUNDER · MOUNTAIN GUIDE Vishal Thakur

FOUNDER · MOUNTAIN GUIDE

Vishal Thakur.

Bhrighu Adventure was founded by Vishal Thakur, a Manali-based mountain guide, climber and mountaineer.

His connection with the mountains developed through years spent trekking, rock climbing, mountaineering and working in outdoor environments.

Bhrighu Adventure grew from that experience into a platform for sharing Himalayan journeys with people who want to experience the mountains beyond ordinary sightseeing.
