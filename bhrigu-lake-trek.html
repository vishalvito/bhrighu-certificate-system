THE JOURNEY

Above the valleys of Manali.

Bhrigu Lake is a high-altitude Himalayan trek starting from the Manali region and travelling through changing mountain terrain towards the lake.

The journey combines forest approaches, open alpine meadows, mountain camps and expansive Himalayan views in a short three-day programme.

Our planned route begins from the Gulaba side and finishes towards Vashisht, creating a journey rather than simply returning by the same trail.

THE ROUTE

Gulaba → Bhrigu Lake → Panduropa → Vashisht

A three-day traverse through the mountain landscape above Manali.

02

DAY BY DAY

3 days in the mountains.

01

DAY ONE

Manali → Gulaba → Mountain Camp

Begin the journey from Manali and travel towards the Gulaba side. From the road head, start trekking into the mountain landscape and gradually gain altitude towards the camping area.

Drive Trekking Camp
02

DAY TWO

Bhrigu Lake → Panduropa

Trek towards Bhrigu Lake through the high-altitude landscape. After reaching the lake area and spending time in the mountains, continue towards Panduropa for the next stage of the journey.

Bhrigu Lake High Altitude Panduropa
03

DAY THREE

Panduropa → Vashisht

Begin the final descent from the mountain camp towards Vashisht. The trail gradually leaves the higher alpine terrain and returns towards the Manali valley, completing the three-day traverse.

Descent Vashisht Finish
