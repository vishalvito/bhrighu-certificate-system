ABOUT THE COURSE

Learn Rock Climbing The Right Way

This 5-day rock climbing course is designed for beginners and intermediate climbers who want to build solid technical foundations, improve movement skills and gain confidence on real rock.

Training combines practical climbing, rope work, belaying, knots, anchors, rappelling and safety systems under professional supervision in the climbing areas around Manali.

5-DAY TRAINING PLAN

From First Move To Confident Climbing

Each day builds on the previous one, combining instruction, practice and real climbing sessions around Manali.

DAY 01
INTRODUCTION & BASICS

Start With Strong Foundations

Understand equipment, basic climbing systems and the safety principles required before getting on the wall.

  • Course introduction and safety briefing
  • Climbing equipment and gear knowledge
  • Harness fitting and helmet checks
  • Basic knots and rope handling
  • Indoor climbing and movement basics
DAY 02
MOVEMENT & TECHNIQUE

Climb With Better Movement

Learn how body position, balance and efficient movement can make climbing safer and more controlled.

  • Footwork and body balance
  • Weight transfer and movement efficiency
  • Top-rope climbing
  • Belaying techniques
  • Communication between climber and belayer
DAY 03
LEAD CLIMBING

Progress To Real Rock

Apply your skills outdoors and begin understanding lead climbing systems, route reading and anchor awareness.

  • Introduction to lead climbing
  • Clipping and rope management
  • Anchor systems and gear placement
  • Route reading
  • Outdoor climbing on natural rock
DAY 04
MULTI-PITCH & RAPPELLING

Build Technical Confidence

Learn controlled rope systems used for longer climbs, rappelling and common climbing safety situations.

  • Multi-pitch climbing basics
  • Rappelling techniques
  • Belay station management
  • Rope safety and communication
  • Basic rescue and safety scenarios
DAY 05
PRACTICE & CERTIFICATION

Put Everything Together

The final day focuses on practical application, assessment and completing the course with your official certificate.

  • Full practical climbing session
  • Technique and safety assessment
  • Course review and feedback
  • Individual improvement guidance
  • Bhrighu Adventure certificate distribution
