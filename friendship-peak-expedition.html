EXPEDITION OVERVIEW

Step Into The High Himalayas.

Friendship Peak is a Himalayan climbing objective in the Manali region of Himachal Pradesh.

This Bhrighu Adventure itinerary is planned as a progressive 6-night, 7-day mountain journey, moving from Manali towards the higher camps through Luhali Thach before the summit attempt.

The programme includes time for approach, acclimatisation, preparation and mountain movement before the summit day.

Duration 6 Nights / 7 Days
Starting Point Manali
State Himachal Pradesh
Route Via Luhali Thach
DAY BY DAY

Expedition Itinerary

01
DAY ONE

Arrival In Manali

Meet in Manali for expedition briefing, equipment review, preparation and discussion of the days ahead.

02
DAY TWO

Manali To Luhali Thach

Begin the mountain approach and move towards Luhali Thach, entering the alpine landscape above the Manali valley.

03
DAY THREE

Acclimatisation & Training

A mountain preparation day focused on acclimatisation, movement, equipment and preparation for higher terrain.

04
DAY FOUR

Move Higher

Continue towards the higher mountain camp while adjusting to altitude and preparing for the summit phase.

05
DAY FIVE

Summit Preparation

Final preparation, equipment checks and rest before the planned summit attempt.

06
SUMMIT DAY

Friendship Peak Attempt

Begin the summit attempt according to mountain, snow and weather conditions. Return towards camp after the climb.

07
DAY SEVEN

Return To Manali

Descend from the mountain and complete the expedition journey back towards Manali.
